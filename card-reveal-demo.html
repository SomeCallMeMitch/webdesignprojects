Frequently asked questions

Before the Curtain Rises

Planning a performance starts with the occasion, your guests and the space.

View the inquiry demo
What types of events can Keith perform at?

Keith’s website lists birthday parties, corporate events and private gatherings.

What kind of magic can guests experience?

Keith describes close-up, walk-around and stage or parlour magic. His repertoire includes cards, coins, illusions and mentalism.

How long does a performance last?

Published packages vary by event. Some pair a short show with a separate close-up or walk-around set. Confirm the running time when discussing your event.

Is the show suitable for children and adults?

Keith describes performances for a range of ages. Share your guests’ ages when discussing the show so you can choose a suitable format.

How much does a performance cost?

Keith’s website describes custom pricing. The date, location, estimated guest count and preferred format are useful details to include when asking for a quote.

What if our date or venue is still tentative?

Start with the details you know. Confirm the date, venue requirements and availability when you discuss the arrangements.
