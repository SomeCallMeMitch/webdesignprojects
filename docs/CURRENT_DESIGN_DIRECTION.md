## Our goal
Create a visually remarkable, high-end, highly interactive **desktop** concept for professional San Francisco magician Keith Tyran / The Gentleman Magician, borrowing the **design principles and interactive staging** of https://hercmagnus.com/ while composing an ORIGINAL Keith-specific treatment. The website concept is speculative/unofficial; Keith has not commissioned, endorsed or provided media rights.

## Latest USER-CONFIRMED direction (Nov 13)
1. **Desktop only now.** Do not design, mock up, audit or plan mobile in this design phase. When mobile gets its own phase, it must be *intentionally and separately designed*, not merely a responsive shrink of the desktop.
2. The key attraction of Herc Magnus is **a layered full-hero visual scene**: a large *transparent-background cutout* of the person floating above the background, plus separate environment/lighting/atmosphere/typography layers.
3. A **prominent draggable slider actually changes the person/pose/appearance**, not simply a background shade. On Herc, rock mode and speaking mode use different pose cutouts, scenes, lighting, supporting text and CTA. **Keith is neither being presented as a musician nor as a speaker.** His slider states must BOTH be relevant to his actual magician identity.
4. **Retain Keith's existing site colors and recognizable brand tone.** Prior live desktop observation: purple header/nav and color language, charcoal/dark, white, green-backed image treatment. This is not a verified list of hexadecimal brand tokens; precise sampling remains open. Avoid arbitrarily substituting burgundy, antique gold, copper or midnight blue.
5. The user **does still want curtains and theatrical lighting** worked into the layered immersive scene as appropriate. Their rejection of earlier 'Velvet Reveal' A1/A2/A3 was a rejection of those generic standalone compositions, **not** an instruction to ban curtains or lights. Lighting can react to hover/pointer, the slider and transitions, but visual priority is the **person-changing slider**.
6. The user suggested **two different Keith appearances, perhaps 'before' and 'after' poses** around a magic moment, as one possible meaningful transformation. This is exploration, not an approved exact pose set or final labels. Treat it as part of MAGIC and performance, not literal rock-versus-speaker imitation.
7. **Planning/design only**, no code, app implementation, deployment, publishing, public concept site, client outreach, asset copying, or unauthorized use of portraits/logos/reviews. ChatGPT Chat/Work for strategy, Codex only upon later explicit approval.

## DESIGN COMPOSITION — proposed, NOT approved
- **Layer 1 — background:** Original stage/magic-performance environment consistent with Keith's purple/charcoal/green/white brand. It may use **velvet/curtains**, subtle set depth and purposeful props, but must not look like Herc's rock concert stage.
- **Layer 2 — light/atmosphere:** Performance-stage lighting, subtle haze/glows, physically plausible spill on curtains and person; pointer-reactive light *may* be added after composition works. Slow, polished transitions rather than an over-animated game.
- **Layer 3 — primary figure:** Large professionally isolated cutout of Keith, significant height and foreground depth. Prepare **two suitably distinct licensed cutouts** with coherent scale/pose; do not mislabel, fake, or download unauthorized photographs. Placeholder art must clearly be a conceptual substitute, not counterfeit client imagery.
- **Layer 4 — UI and copy:** Name, concise message, meaningful slider labels, visible `Request availability` contact CTA. The CTA is an inquiry, not verified live availability. Both states communicate something real about Keith.
- **Slider:** Compare exactly what changes (portrait, expression, pose, prop, lighting, maybe curtain opening/background and contextual copy). A transition between *before the magic* and *after the reveal* is an idea; alternative 'calm gentleman ↔ playful showman' or 'close-up ↔ stage' may fit if documented photos/offer permit. User needs to select the actual narrative.
- **Depth goal:** Strong enough to be WOW but spatially credible and not a photo cutout over a flat gradient. Separate position, edge treatment, ambient light, shadows and backdrop must form a believable scene.
- **Never copy Herc's guitar, amps, exact stage scenery, proprietary graphics, music, slider art or source code.** Match the interaction *quality and principles*, not copyrighted assets/composition.
- Avoid generic top-to-bottom dark velvet invitation/card design that user explicitly rejected.
