## Design QA — crucial distinctions before approval

1. **Creative drift:** The render **visually follows concept B 'Gentleman vs playful persona'**, **not** the previously described concept A 'The Impossible Moment' magical before/after event. This is a proposed comparison visual, not the promised validated A design or a user-chosen direction. Must flag this to user and not quietly rewrite their choice.
2. **Static visualization:** The screenshot depicts a middle/split state; it does **not** demonstrate that slider works or how two complete pose states really animate. Do not imply code or actual interaction exists.
3. **Face seam:** Splitting a nearly identical face down its center can feel like a before/after photo wipe, whereas the requested Herc inspiration changes the foreground **pose/appearance of the entire figure**. A more compelling future pair needs explicitly different body gestures and different whole-portrait alpha cutouts, aligned in 3D scene and lighting. Avoid stitched face or uncanny overlapped fingers in actual slider.
4. **Generic magic clichés:** Hat, floating cards and glowing effects are aesthetically recognizable but could undermine a more original theatrical magician interaction. User previously rejected unrelated card gimmicks; do not infer this artwork/prop direction is approved.
5. **Wardrobe risk:** Red leather can read musician/rock persona rather than his actual magician work. Photographer's March reference displayed such a look, but this does not establish a distinct marketed 'playful magician' product or its licensing.
6. **Brand and rights:** The rendered emblem/typography are generated, **not** Keith's authorized logo or exact brand; do not imply trademark rights from the rendered tiny 'TM'. Generated figure is a **concept stand-in**, not verified likeness of Keith or usable final portrait. Real portraits/act props require rights and validation. Purple reference is grounded in source but exact official brand hex remains to verify.
7. **Conversion:** Keep the hero headline, clear site purpose and 'Request Availability' action legible independent of slider; the CTA should be a real inquiry only if later authorized to implement.
8. **Scene realism:** Future comps should establish real stage floor/horizon, figure contact shadows, grounded key/rim/bounce lighting, controlled overlapping curtain layers and realistic depth; spectacle should not rely only on haze/glow.
