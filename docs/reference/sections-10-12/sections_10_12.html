01What types of events can Keith perform at?

Keith’s current website describes birthday parties, corporate events and private gatherings. Tell us about your occasion to explore a suitable performance.

02What kind of magic can guests experience?

Keith describes close-up and walk-around entertainment, stage or parlour performances, cards, coins, illusions and mentalism. The right combination depends on your event.

03How long does a performance last?

His published examples vary by occasion, sometimes combining a show with walk-around or close-up magic. Duration and format would be confirmed as part of a real inquiry.

04Is the entertainment for children or adults?

His public descriptions include entertainment for different age groups. Including your guest ages helps begin a conversation about the most appropriate show.

05How much does a performance cost?

Keith’s website describes customized pricing. A date, location, approximate guest count and requested style help establish a quote.

06What if our date or venue is not final?

You can describe tentative plans in this concept form. Actual availability, venue requirements and booking arrangements would need to be confirmed with Keith.
