Date: 2026-11-15 (America/Los_Angeles). **Planning only. These are proposals, not approved implementation.**

## Current user decision

The user reports that Experience’s moving lights work great. Preserve that successful interaction and the upper sections; the main hero remains locked.

The Show smoke dissolve does not work well: it is much smaller than the card and appears/disappears as a disconnected puff. Do not treat the Stage A technical-readiness verdict as visual approval. Alpha validity, matching dimensions and successful controls did not establish a convincing transformation. Current smoke remains in its preserved review version until a specific change/removal is requested; no runtime edit is part of this ideas task.

A later smoke retry would need coverage over the entire chosen surface (illustration versus whole framed card must be decided), bottom-up growth, enough opacity at the swap moment, and coordinated reveal/dissipation. A larger static puff alone will not establish coherent rising/billowing motion. A properly directed alpha sequence or a convincingly animated layered/masked version needs its own one-card visual test. This task does not commission it.

## Recommended next pair

Prototype **the enchanted seal** and **the footer ghostlight** in copied modules. They are separated down the page, clearly discoverable, and do different things. If a test without new raster art is preferred, invisible ink and the runaway shadow are the cheapest starting points.

Build at most two, not this whole list. The watch, escaping ace and curtain bow are alternative footer concepts, not additions to stack around the lamp. The larger paper unfold is a later choice after real review content/layers are ready.

## Shared behavior requirements for any later authorized build

- A discovery can react to pointer proximity/hover; information or a deliberate replay uses a real button/link and keyboard focus/activation. Do not add arbitrary focus stops to static decoration.
- One active discovery, brief gesture, reset on exit without flicker or queued replays. A card reveal may wait for a short intentional dwell; exact timing belongs to the copied prototype.
- Reduced motion provides static illumination/direct reveal. Clear offscreen/hidden/resize work and wait for decoded art. Never hide essential text or make a booking route depend on discovering an Easter egg.
- No new autoplay sound, site-wide cursor trail, continuous particle field or automatic gallery motion. Keep physical origins credible; existing painted beams remain stationary outside the already prepared Experience copy.
- Read-only asset reuse does not authorize editing hero assets, loading hero.js again or changing baseline/v1/shared originals. Preserve the current older experiments and recipes. No live publication until a requested implementation is complete and follows the existing private-review workflow.
- Existing labels remain: unofficial concept, concept artwork, placeholder reviews and local-only inquiry. Decorative writing is not Keith’s authenticated autograph or a real review; no fake endorsement or booking confirmation.
- Any implementation must test the actual three-size desktop layout, hover/keyboard/repeat/rapid exit, reduced motion, image readiness, offscreen/visibility, integrity and zero-send form. This planning task ran no new browser/performance tests.
