## Proposed desktop page flow
1. **Grand Entrance — existing hero.** Preserve approved art/behavior. Transition to next section through visual stage-lip/curtain-shadow handoff rather than a blunt hard cut. No edits in this task.
2. **Experience the Magic — new intro/visual proof.** One strong performance image or optional consent-cleared 20–40 sec click-to-play clip, with smaller audience-reaction visual and concise value proposition. Options: A) spotlight reveal (recommended); B) mild living-photo depth; C) miniature curtain reveal. Avoid autoplay video/audio or falsely attributed reactions. **High priority; low–medium complexity.**
3. **The Show — redesign six skills.** Six small thematic interactive displays for Illusions, Cards & Coins, Mentalism, Comedy, Storytelling, Mind Reading. Each uses one micro-effect on hover/focus/click and reveals clear text explaining the category. Example decorative motifs: hat shimmer, floating card/coin, mystic sphere, comic flourish, book opening, sealed prediction. Symbolic only—do not imply unverified actual tricks. Alternative six flipping oversized cards. Recommend thematic displays for variety (avoid repeating hero cards). **High; medium.**
4. **Choose Your Event — redesign entertainment options.** Three tall theatrical playbills/posters for Birthdays, Corporate Events, Private Events, each with relevant factual format detail and a request-availability action. Hover adds soft light sweep/lift; never hides essential information. Differentiate this section (occasions) from The Show (techniques). Do not invent weddings as active package unless confirmed; wedding variant may be offered conditionally for other performers. **High; low.**
5. **Behind the Curtain — About Keith.** Large rights-cleared portrait, concise genuine story, warm personality and professional context. Select one restrained effect: portrait spotlight (recommended), slight curtain part, or a more-info panel. Do not fabricate personal history, awards or stage anecdotes. **Medium–high; low.**
6. **Trusted by Audiences — client credibility.** Refined fixed logo/name grid framed like subtle theater hall-of-recognition, with hover highlights. Only verified and permitted logos/names; avoid projecting endorsement. Alternative gentle scrolling marquee, less preferred than stable clear proof. Consider platform proof links separately. **High; low.**
7. **Moments of Wonder — gallery redesign.** Reuse real permitted performance photos; prefer a manual horizontal filmstrip mixing image aspect ratios and descriptive captions over nonstop square autoplay. Three treatments:
   - **A Spotlight Discovery (recommended):** full thumbnails remain recognizable by default; hover/focus adds a bounded circular brightening/soft glow, click opens accessible lightbox.
   - **B Magic Card Flip:** frames turn to reveal caption/second real image; useful for playful magician variant, but repetitive with Keith hero.
   - **C Floating Gallery:** hovered frame rises forward as neighbors recede, dramatic but heavier and more difficult.
   Choose A with a small amount of C's depth. Keyboard focus must trigger equivalent affordance; pause/stop motion and no uncontrolled auto-scroll. **Very high; medium.**
8. **Audience Reactions & Testimonials — new/expanded.** Two or three verified reviews styled as audience letters or theater programs; possible unfolding highlight on hover/focus, click to read full review/source. Link to real review source as appropriate; no fake or unattributed invented testimonials. Alternate straightforward review carousel for more corporate magicians. **High; low–medium.**
9. **How Booking Works — new.** Proposed flow: event details -> discuss fit/performance -> confirm arrangements. Presented as three linked theater admission tickets with subtle progressive glow. This is a design explanation, not a verified Keith-specific booking process or promised response SLA. Verify real process with client before production. **High; low.**
10. **FAQ — new.** Accessible theater-program accordions covering event suitability, close-up versus stage, durations, guest ages, venue, area served, setup and custom pricing. All operational promises need confirmation; currently public website says custom quote. No closed information traps or animations blocking reading. **Medium; low.**
11. **Your Invitation Awaits — contact/inquiry redesign.** Final invitation/ticket motif, clear Request Availability CTA, concise demo form. Suggested initial fields: date (permit undecided), location, event type, name, email; optional guest count, phone and notes. Present as *inquiry*, not booked availability. In an unofficial demo no real sending and no false success. Verify destination, consent, spam controls and privacy before any future authorized integration. **Critical; low–medium.**
12. **Final Curtain — footer.** Simple elegant brand/navigation/contact/legal footer with faint fabric texture and maybe an unobtrusive dim-to-rest effect. Do not add a second major animation. **Low; low.**

## Suggested visual rhythm
- Section 1 is the principal theatrical interaction. Use a stage-to-backstage transition for Section 2.
- Give the viewer a breathing space between major animated sections. Limit elaborate effects to hero, Show displays, Gallery. Make other sections largely static with small light/glow responses.
- Maintain purple/charcoal/white brand continuity and tasteful gilded details as a background decorative material; do not make the entire site relentlessly high-saturation or copy a reference site's art.
- Keep readable headings and actual HTML copy, visible direct inquiry links, high contrast and user-controlled motion.
- The high-value conversion information—types of events, proof, performance formats, request—should be discoverable with motion disabled.

## Reusable magician spec-site platform (design intent)
Re-use **components and interaction primitives**, not the exact same visual website:
- Configurable section building blocks: hero, performance formats, events, biography, proof, gallery, reviews, how-it-works, FAQ, inquiry.
- Reusable micro-interactions: spotlight hover, curtain peek, prop reveal, card turnover, picture depth, letter unfold, ticket progression.
- Three optional visual systems: **Grand Theater** (Keith: elegant proscenium), **Modern Impossible** (minimal geometric illusion and clean typography), **Close-Up Lounge** (warm intimate props and audience focus). Future variants may add colorful family-oriented layouts as appropriate.
- Let each site change section order, category count, fonts, color tokens, imagery, specialty and signature interaction. The user explicitly does **not** want identical sites with only superficial recoloring.
- Use original licensed media and copy per prospect; no automatic acquisition/reuse of photos, reviews, trademarks, recordings or endorsements without rights.
