# Project brief — Keith Tyran interactive magician website concept

**Status:** early concept and research only. **Branch:** `concept/keith-tyran-magician-experience`. **Updated:** 2026-11-13.

## Objective
Develop an unusually memorable, elegant, theatrical and interactive website concept for magician Keith Tyran, inspired by the reactive stage-lighting, stateful visual changes and playful interactive controls of https://hercmagnus.com/. This is an **unofficial speculative design**, not an existing engagement or a replacement authorized by Keith.

## Why this prospect
- Keith Tyran, branded as 'The Gentleman Magician', has an existing site: https://www.keithtyran.com/
- Public profiles and prior research suggest an established performing/booking business. Verified facts, cited sources and uncertain counts are tracked in PROSPECT_RESEARCH.
- Magic is an especially apt field for interactive storytelling: surprises, illusion, reveals, controlled light, cards, tasteful showmanship.

## Intended business use
Create a persuasive proof of concept for a custom web-design business. Avoid building the final client website before a design review and permission to use client materials.

## Desired experience (initial exploration, not approved specifications)
- Dramatic but sophisticated entrance; elegant theatrical atmosphere, not a generic children's magic website.
- Mouse/pointer reactive lighting or subtle stage spotlight.
- Small opt-in interactive 'magic moment' (e.g. chosen card/vanishing object) without obstructing content or booking.
- Smooth, cinematic reveals of stage magic, close-up magic, mentalism, proof and reviews.
- Readily accessible booking inquiry CTA throughout, especially on mobile.
- A site that impresses potential buyers while still loading quickly and meeting accessibility needs.
- Potentially a reusable architecture for other performers, with prospect-specific art and interactions.

## Outcomes to plan, not yet implement
1. Prospect and audience validation: booking customer segments, services, distinctive positioning and credible public evidence.
2. Comparative audit of existing site (desktop/mobile), competitors, exemplary interactive artist sites.
3. Three differentiated creative directions with measurable advantages and disadvantages.
4. Content hierarchy, booking path and interaction map for desktop/mobile.
5. Asset licensing plan and clear separation between mock demo assets and production assets.
6. One prototype scope with acceptance criteria, performance/accessibility budgets and milestones.
7. Codex implementation handoff after approval.

## Boundaries
- No copying existing site source/assets or Herc Magnus creative assets.
- No prospect contact or publishing without explicit user approval.
- No technical stack chosen yet; consider plain HTML/CSS/JS, Astro, React/Vite or Next based on requirements, not fashion.
- No claim of confirmed paid conversions or booking revenue from public signals.
- Tracking and decisions live in AGENTS, PROGRESS, DESIGN_PREFERENCES and companion research docs.
