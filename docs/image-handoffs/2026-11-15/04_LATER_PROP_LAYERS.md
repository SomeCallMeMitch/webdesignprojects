# Optional later layers — do not generate in Stage A

These briefs preserve the ambitious options without asking the next chat to generate an entire library at once. The user must select a later effect before making its layers.

## Common rules

- Each layer is its own true-alpha PNG, registered on the same canvas for that specific prop, with a manifest of dimensions and pivot/occlusion notes. No collaged layers or labels inside images.
- Keep the original artwork unchanged; new hidden surfaces, repaired backgrounds or alternate poses are newly generated concept art, not recovered original content.
- Do not claim identical alignment between separately generated images without comparing/re-registering them. When possible, derive layers from one approved master/edit rather than regenerating the whole scene for every part.
- Do not enlarge 505px source art and claim new original detail. Ask for a larger newly painted master only when the selected effect needs it, then report it honestly as a derivative.
- Code supplies text, controls, motion, masks, occlusion and reduced-motion alternatives. Images must not bake those in.

## 1. Storytelling book

Reference: `png-references/assets/section03_show/show_05_storytelling.png`, currently an OPEN flat illustrated book.

For a real opening/page-turn scene, prepare a consistent new animation-ready master inspired by that book, then separate: clean backdrop without book; book body/spine; front cover; back cover if visible; left/right page stacks; one independent turning page with front/back surfaces; warm gutter light overlay; soft cast shadow. A closed starting pose and hidden surfaces are new art, because the source only shows the open result. Register pivots at the actual spine. Start with 2D/limited perspective rather than promising a full 3D book from one picture.

## 2. Illusion cabinet

Reference: `.../section03_show/show_01_illusions.png`.

Separate new copies into: clean stage backdrop; cabinet body with openable area reconstructed as an interior; independent lid/door; matching inside face of the lid/door; hinge/contact shadow; restrained interior light. Keep the visible cabinet design, camera and palette consistent. Hidden interior/backside surfaces must be identified as new concept art. Smoke comes from Stage A; do not bake it into cabinet parts. First decide whether the selected effect is a lid or front-door opening, since they require different geometry.

## 3. Letter unfolds

Reference: `.../section08_reactions/review_letter_blank.png` (700x875 RGBA).

Prepare: independent envelope back; envelope front/pocket; flap; seal if the chosen motion uses it; blank paper in compatible folded panels; soft contact shadows; any fixed decorative frame separately. Keep all actual review text out of the bitmap. The HTML review must move with its paper surface. The present single image combines paper/envelope/seal/frame; moving only its text does not make a real unfolding letter.

## 4. Floating coin / card

Reference: `.../section03_show/show_02_cards_and_coins.png`.

For coin movement, supply coin-free repaired backdrop/hand/card scene, isolated coin front, and matching back/edge surfaces only if an actual spin is chosen. Keep fingers/anatomy intact; removing a coin may require newly painted hidden skin. A single coin-front cutout supports translation/small tilt, not a convincing unrestricted 3D rotation. Avoid duplicating the hero's signature card transformation across every exhibit.

## 5. Crystal eye

Reference: `.../section03_show/show_06_mind_reading.png`.

Prepare a base ball/pedestal without the eye, isolated iris/pupil or complete eye layer depending on the selected gesture, an interior haze layer, and a separate front glass/reflection layer/mask to place above the eye. Eye remains visually inside the glass. Keep the locked hero's separate crystal-ball/seer assets untouched.

## 6. About portrait / small curtains

References: `.../section05_behind_curtain/magician_portrait_concept.png` and `.../section05_behind_curtain/behind_curtain_atmosphere.png`.

For depth, derive a transparent concept-person cutout plus a repaired empty backdrop. Preserve appearance and keep the result labeled as concept imagery, not an authenticated portrait/photo. For parting curtains, provide independent left/right curtain panels with appropriate folds and alpha edges. Do not regenerate or move the actual hero figure or its curtains.

## 7. Performance-preview curtains

Use the Section 02 theater backdrop for style. Generate independent left/right small curtain panels and optional static top valance on the same registered player canvas. No fake video footage or playback UI in the images. Real approved footage or selected permitted stills are a separate content input; the current preview has no performance video.
