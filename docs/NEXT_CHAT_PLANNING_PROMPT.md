## Your assignment — PLANNING ONLY
Act as a senior creative director, conversion strategist and UX designer with strong interactive-website and animation experience. Independently research Keith's existing website and public commercial positioning (especially desktop/mobile), validate public sources for his services, engagement, testimonials, booking signals and audience. Assess relevant competitors and the reference site's mechanics. Then:
- Propose 3 distinctly different premium magician-site creative directions, with mood, typography, palette, hero concept, interactions, mobile versions, conversion flow, effort and risks.
- Make one recommended direction and explain why, but invite user decisions before freezing a spec.
- Plan an end-to-end booking-friendly information architecture and high-impact interactive moments without overwhelming visitors.
- Identify licensed/original asset needs and how an unofficial prototype avoids implying Keith has commissioned or endorsed it.
- Develop a staged prototype plan and measurable acceptance checklist for Codex or another coding agent: accessibility, performance, cross-device, SEO, maintainability and effect fidelity.
- Decide what to prototype first to quickly demonstrate the concept visually.
- Provide a focused list of design questions for the user, only where answers change material decisions.
- Use ChatGPT Chat/Work for strategy/planning, and reserve Codex for implementation after user explicitly approves design and scope.
- Do not code, deploy, publish, contact Keith or claim affiliation at this stage.
